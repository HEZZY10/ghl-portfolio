Post-Call Follow-Up Sequence (14 days)

The problem: After a sales call, prospects went cold because follow-up depended on someone remembering to send it.

What I built: A GoHighLevel workflow that starts within 2 hours of the call. It combines automated emails, SMS messages timed 20 minutes to 3 hours after each email, a personal advisor email for the human touch, and smart waits across 14 days. At the end it swaps tags automatically so the contact moves to the right next stage.

Result: Every lead gets a consistent follow-up with no manual chasing. (Add real numbers here if you have them.)

30-Day Email and SMS Nurture Sequence

The problem: Leads needed education over time, not one email and silence.

What I built: A pipeline-triggered workflow. When an opportunity moves to a specific stage, it starts a 30-day journey of weekly themed emails, with SMS reminders and waits between them.

Result: Leads are nurtured automatically from the moment they enter the stage.

Appointment Booking Alerts

The problem: The team was finding out about new bookings late, and clients got no instant confirmation.

What I built: A workflow that fires the moment someone books on a calendar. It sends the client an instant confirmation SMS, texts the team member, sends an internal notification, and tags the contact for tracking.

Result: Instant confirmations for clients and instant alerts for the team.
